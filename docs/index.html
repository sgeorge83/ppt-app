Privacy Policy — WordOnAir Slides

Effective date: November 4, 2026
App: WordOnAir Slides (Windows)
Publisher: WordOnAir Labs · wordonair.com

WordOnAir Slides is a free Windows app that places World English Bible and Urdu Geo verses on PowerPoint slides for pastors. It works without an account, without advertising, and without collecting personal information.

1. Information the app handles

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, or a Microsoft account.

The church name, chosen theme, language, and the path to a logo you pick are stored only on this PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WordOnAirLabs\WordOnAirSlides\settings.json. The logo file itself stays where you saved it. Those details are written into the PowerPoint file you create, because that is the slide you asked for.

The World English Bible and the Urdu Geo Version are included in the app. Looking up a verse does not send the reference to WordOnAir Labs.

WordOnAir Labs does not sell personal information and does not use advertising IDs.

2. Why that information is stored

  • To remember the church name, logo, theme, and language the next time you open the app.
  • To put that church name and logo on the PowerPoint slides you create.

3. Permissions

The app does not request internet access, camera, microphone, location, or contacts. It reads a logo image only when you choose one, and it writes the PowerPoint file you ask it to save.

4. Scripture

World English Bible text is public domain. Urdu Geo Version text is included under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. The app copies the wording and does not rewrite it. Slides that include Urdu credit “Urdu Geo Version · World English Bible”.

5. Children

WordOnAir Slides is a general-audience religious text tool. It has no ads and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
